Frequently asked questions

FAQ

It is recommended to have an initial orthodontic consultation around the age of 6 or 7. At that age, certain jaw growth issues can be detected early and addressed if needed.

The duration depends on the complexity of the case, typically ranging from 12 to 24 months. During your initial consultation, we will provide a precise estimate tailored to your individual situation.

The first few days after fitting or an adjustment may cause some mild discomfort. This is temporary and easily managed with a simple pain reliever. Modern techniques have greatly reduced any inconvenience.

We offer clear aligners (transparent trays), multi-bracket braces, as well as interceptive treatments for children.

There is no age limit for orthodontic treatment. Many discreet options are available for adults, including clear aligners and multi-bracket braces.

Follow-up appointments are scheduled on average every 6 to 12 weeks, depending on the type of treatment and its progress. Each visit lasts between 15 and 30 minutes.

Thorough brushing after every meal is essential. For aligners, they should be removed before eating and cleaned regularly. We will provide you with detailed care instructions specific to your appliance.
