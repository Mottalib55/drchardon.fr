Interceptive
Orthodontics

Early interceptive treatment to guide jaw growth and prevent future complications. The sooner we act, the simpler the treatment.

Why consult during childhood?

Interceptive orthodontics aims to correct jaw growth abnormalities and guide the eruption of permanent teeth while a child is still actively growing. This is the most favorable window for intervention, as the developing bones respond readily to gentle orthodontic forces.

A first consultation is recommended around age 6 to 7, when the first permanent teeth begin to come through. This does not necessarily mean that treatment will be started right away, but it allows a thorough assessment that can identify potential issues early on.

Benefits of early treatment

Guides healthy jaw growth and development
May prevent the need for extractions later on
Shorter and simpler treatment in adolescence
Addresses harmful habits such as thumb sucking
Improves nasal breathing patterns
Builds confidence during formative years

Treatment process

01

Initial assessment

A child-friendly clinical examination, appropriate imaging, and an analysis of jaw growth patterns.

02

Tailored appliance

A custom-made device is selected for your child, such as a removable appliance or palatal expander, chosen to suit their age and specific needs.

03

Growth monitoring

Regular appointments to adjust the appliance and track how growth and tooth development are progressing.

04

Observation phase

After the active phase, she monitors the eruption of the remaining permanent teeth and evaluate whether any additional treatment will be needed during adolescence.
