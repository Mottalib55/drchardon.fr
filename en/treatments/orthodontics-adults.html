Orthodontics at any age

Orthodontic treatment is not limited to children and teenagers. A growing number of adults are choosing to address alignment and bite issues, whether for aesthetic, functional, or oral health reasons. Crooked or crowded teeth can make cleaning difficult and contribute to gum disease, while bite imbalances may cause jaw discomfort or uneven wear over time.

she offers solutions specifically tailored to adult patients: clear aligners and multi-bracket braces. Each treatment plan is designed to integrate into your daily routine with minimal disruption, so that your professional and social life carry on as usual.
