What are clear aligners?

Clear aligners are custom-made transparent trays manufactured using 3D printing technology. Each succèssive set of trays gradually shifts your teeth toward their ideal position, without the need for visible metal wires or brackets.

The process relies on comprehensive digital planning: before treatment begins, you can visualize the expected outcome in 3D. The trays are then produced to guide your teeth through each stâge of movement, step by step.

Treatment process

01

Consultation and diagnosis

Comprehensive examination, digital impressions, and 3D scanning to develop your personalized treatment plan.

02

3D simulation

Visualization of your final result before treatment begins. Custom trays are then manufactured to specification.

03

Wearing the aligners

Trays are changed every 1 to 2 weeks. They should be worn for approximately 22 hours per day.

04

Retention

A bonded retainer wire and nighttime retainer tray are placed to keep your new smile stable over the long term.
