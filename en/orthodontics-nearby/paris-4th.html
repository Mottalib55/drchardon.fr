The 4th arrondissement and its residents

The 4th arrondissement lies at the very heart of Paris, straddling both banks of the Seine. It encompasses some of the most visited sites in France, beginning with Notre-Dame de Paris, the medieval cathedral whose restoration following the 2019 fire has captivated the world. The Ile de la Cite, historical cradle of Paris, also houses the Sainte-Chapelle, the Conciergerie and the Palais de Justice. Across the Pont Saint-Louis, the Ile Saint-Louis préservés its timeless elegance with its 17th-century town houses, its artisan shops and its famous ice cream parlour Berthillon.

The southern part of the Marais constitutes the other major area of the 4th arrondissement. The Place des Vosges, the oldest planned square in Paris inaugurated in 1612, remains one of the most beautiful locations in the capital. Its brick and stone arcades shelter galleries, restaurants and the Maison de Victor Hugo. The surrounding streets, including the Rue des Francs-Bourgeois and the Rue de Rivoli, concentrate a remarkable blend of luxury boutiques, independent shops and historic buildings. The Centre Pompidou, with its distinctive exposed-structure architecture, attracts millions of visitors each year for its collections of modern and contemporary art.

Beneath its tourist surface, the 4th arrondissement is also a residential neighbourhood with a deeply-rooted local life. The residents of the Ile Saint-Louis, the Place des Vosges area and the streets around the Hotel de Ville form a close-knit community. Families, young professionals and retirees coexist in buildings that have seen centuries of Parisian history. The Hotel de Ville (Paris City Hall), which dominates the western side of the arrondissement, is both a political centre and a gathering place for exhibitions, ice skating in winter and various events throughout the year.

The population of the 4th is culturally diverse and well-informed. Many of her patients from this area are professionals who value discretion and efficiency. They choose clear aligners that allow them to pursue their careers without any visible orthodontic appliance, or they bring their children for early orthodontic assessments, understanding the importance of timely intervention.

Orthodontic treatments offered to patients from the 4th

Dr Chardon's practice is exclusively dedicated to orthodontics, enabling her to maintain the highest level of expertise across all techniques. Clear aligners are extremely popular with patients from the 4th arrondissement, who appreciate the combination of invisibility and convenience. Treatment process for a patient from the 4th arrondissement

The journey begins with a comprehensive initial consultation at the Colombes practice. Dr Chardon carries out a full clinical examination, takes intraoral and extraoral photographs, panoramic X-rays and digital impressions using a state-of-the-art intraoral scanner. She then présents a détailed treatment plan, explaining each option, the expected timeline, the number of appointments and the total cost. Complete transparency is a cornerstone of her approach: patients leave the first appointment with all the information they need to make an informed décision, with no pressure to commit immédiately.

Once treatment begins, follow-up appointments are spaced every 6 to 12 weeks. Each session lasts 15 to 30 minutes. Dr Chardon verifies that tooth movements are progressing according to plan, adjusts the applied forces and ensures that oral hygiene is properly maintained. For patients from the 4th, this means a trip to Colombes roughly once every two months, easily accommodated during a lunch break or after work. She offers morning, early afternoon and late afternoon slots to suit varying professional schedules.

The retention phase is a critical step that she considers as important as the active treatment itself. Regular checks during the first year after appliance removal confirm the long-term stability of the results. Her commitment to each patient extends well beyond the last active appointment.
