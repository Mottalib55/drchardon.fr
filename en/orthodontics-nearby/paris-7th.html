Orthodontic treatments available

My practice offers a comprehensive suite of orthodontic treatments, each selected and adapted based on a thorough diagnostic evaluation. I believe in matching the treatment to the patient, not the other way around.

Clear aligners are a refined, discreet option for correcting misaligned teeth. These custom-made transparent trays are worn over the teeth and replaced at prescribed intervals to progressively shift the teeth into alignment. For residents of the 7th arrondissement -- many of whom hold positions in diplomacy, government, finance, or the arts -- the invisibility of aligners is a significant advantage. They can be removed for meals and for oral hygiene, which eliminates the dietary restrictions associated with fixed braces and makes it easy to maintain excellent dental health throughout treatment.

Multi-bracket braces provide the highest level of orthodontic control and are essential for complex cases. Severe crowding, significant skeletal discrepancies, impacted teeth, and challenging bite relationships are all situations where braces deliver results that other methods cannot match. I use modern bracket systems in both metal and ceramic. Ceramic brackets are designed to be inconspicuous, as they closely match the colour of natural teeth. For adult patients from the 7th who require braces, ceramic brackets offer a balance between clinical effectiveness and visual discretion.

Interceptive orthodontics targets young children, usually between the ages of 6 and 10. Early intervention can address crossbites, narrow palates, excessive overbites, and habits such as thumb-sucking that interfere with normal dental development. By treating these issues during the growth phase, I can often reduce the complexity and duration of later treatment. However, early treatment is only recommended when it offers a clear benefit -- in many cases, the right approach is to observe and wait. I always explain the rationale for my recommendation to parents.

Teenage orthodontics is comprehensive treatment undertaken once most or all permanent teeth have erupted, typically between ages 11 and 16. This is the most common period for orthodontic treatment, and the options include both fixed braces and aligner therapy. I discuss the merits and limitations of each approach with the teenager and their parents, and I encourage the teenager to participate actively in the decision.

Adult orthodontics serves patients who seek correction at any stage of adult life. The motivations are varied: some want to improve the aesthetics of their smile, others are addressing functional problems such as uneven wear or difficulty cleaning crowded teeth, and some have been referred by their dentist for orthodontic preparation before restorative work. I treat adults of all ages and tailor the approach to each patient's specific goals, lifestyle, and clinical profile.

Treatment process

My approach to treatment follows a structured, patient-centred process. The first step is an initial consultation, during which I conduct a thorough clinical examination. I assess tooth alignment, bite relationships, jaw symmetry, and facial balance. I also discuss your concerns, your priorities, and any relevant medical or dental history. This visit establishes the foundation for everything that follows.

Next, I gather diagnostic records: digital impressions, panoramic and cephalometric X-rays, and detailed photographs. I study these records meticulously to develop a treatment plan that addresses your specific needs. At a dedicated planning appointment, I present the plan in full: the recommended technique, the anticipated duration of treatment, the frequency of visits, and a complete, transparent cost breakdown. I make sure you understand every element of the proposal and encourage you to take whatever time you need to consider it.

Once treatment is underway, you return to the practice every 6 to 12 weeks for adjustment or monitoring appointments. These visits are focused and efficient, typically lasting 20 to 30 minutes. At each one, I update you on your progress and discuss any adjustments to the plan. For patients travelling from the 7th arrondissement, these regular visits can be scheduled at times that minimise disruption -- many patients combine their appointment with a lunch break or a morning slot before heading to work.

When active treatment concludes, I transition to the retention phase. Bonded retainers are fixed to the inner surfaces of the front teeth, and removable retainer trays are provided for nighttime wear. Retention is the phase that secures your results for the long term, and I take it as seriously as the active treatment itself. Periodic follow-up appointments allow me to verify that the teeth remain stable in their corrected positions.
