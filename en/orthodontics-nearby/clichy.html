Orthodontics in Clichy

Dr Chardon's practice in Colombes is accessible in 10 minutes by car from Clichy.

Your dental surgeon in Colombes, near Clichy

Clichy sits at the very edge of Paris, a commune that straddles the boundary between the capital and its northern suburbs. If you are among the tens of thousands of people who call Clichy home, orthodontic care is closer than you might expect. Her practice in Colombes is only ten minutes away by car, and she is a dental surgeon whose practice is limited to orthodontics. That description is not a formality -- it defines the way she works. she does not perform fillings, extractions, or cosmetic dentistry. Her entire clinical output is focused on the diagnosis and treatment of orthodontic conditions: misaligned teeth, incorrect bites, jaw disharmonies, and everything that falls under the umbrella of tooth movement and occlusal correction.

This singular focus means she sees a high volume of orthodontic cases every year, across all age groups and levels of complexity. she invests continuously in technology and training, and her practice is equipped with digital imaging, intra-oral scanning, and computer-assisted treatment planning. For patients from Clichy, the result is access to specialist-level orthodontic care with a journey that barely takes longer than crossing the commune itself. Appointments are scheduled every 6 to 12 weeks during active treatment, so the commitment is both measured and manageable.

How to reach the practice from Clichy

Driving from Clichy to her practice in Colombes takes about ten minutes. The most direct route crosses the Pont de Clichy over the Seine and follows the embankments or the Rue Paul Vaillant-Couturier through Asnieres before entering Colombes. An alternative for those in the western part of Clichy is to head through Levallois-Perret and then north through Bois-Colombes into Colombes. Both routes are reliable, and parking near the practice is available on the surrounding residential streets.

By public transport, Clichy is well served by metro line 13, with stations at Mairie de Clichy and metro line 14 at Saint-Ouen. From Mairie de Clichy, you can take metro line 13 toward Saint-Lazare and then connect to the Transilien L line, which brings you directly to the Gare du Stade in Colombes. The total journey time is approximately eighteen minutes. Bus line 340, which runs between Clichy and the northern suburbs, can also be used in combination with other routes. For cyclists, the flat terrain along the Seine makes a bike ride from Clichy to Colombes a pleasant option of about fifteen minutes.

Clichy and its residents

Clichy, or Clichy-la-Garenne to give it its full official name, is a city of around 63,000 inhabitants tucked against the north-western edge of the Paris ring road. It is bordered by the 17th arrondissement of Paris to the south and east, Levallois-Perret to the south-west, Asnieres-sur-Seine to the north, and Saint-Ouen to the east. The Boulevard Victor Hugo, one of the main commercial arteries, is always lively, and the Place de la Mairie serves as the civic and social heart of the town.

Clichy has experienced substantial urban renewal in recent years, particularly in the Quartier du Bac d'Asnieres and the zones around the Boulevard Jean Jaures. New residential developments have brought a wave of younger inhabitants, including many families with children. The city has strong educational infrastructure, with schools such as the Groupe Scolaire Jean Mace and the College Jean Perrin, and it offers a respectable range of cultural and recreational amenities, including the Theatre Rutebeuf and the Piscine Municipale. The Parc Roger Salengro, the main green space, provides a welcome retreat from the busy streets.

The population of Clichy is socially and culturally diverse, and that diversity is reflected in her patient base. she treats children from Clichy who need early interceptive work to correct jaw growth issues, teenagers who require comprehensive orthodontic treatment, and adults who want to improve their smile or resolve bite problems that have persisted for years. The ten-minute journey to Colombes means that none of these patients needs to travel far for high-quality orthodontic care.

Orthodontic treatments for patients from Clichy

Her practice provides the full range of orthodontic treatment options. For young children from Clichy, interceptive orthodontics deals with problems like crossbites, severely protruding upper teeth, or significant crowding of the developing dentition. These interventions are typically carried out between the ages of six and eleven and last six to twelve months. The goal is to guide jaw growth and create conditions for the permanent teeth to erupt in better positions, potentially reducing the need for complex treatment later.

Adolescents from Clichy most often receive multi-bracket braces. she uses self-ligating bracket systems that allow for efficient, low-friction tooth movement. Ceramic brackets, which match the colour of the teeth, are available for those who want a more discreet option. For adults, clear aligners are frequently the treatment of choice. These nearly invisible, removable trays move the teeth incrementally and are designed using digital scans and sophisticated planning software. Adults from Clichy, particularly those in professional roles, find aligners appealing because they are almost undetectable during conversation.

Every treatment recommendation she makes is grounded in a careful clinical assessment. she does not fit aligners where braces would serve the patient better, and she is always straightforward about what each technique can and cannot achieve. Her commitment is to the best possible outcome, regardless of which tool gets us there.

Treatment process for a patient from Clichy

Your treatment begins with a first consultation. she examines your teeth, your bite, and the overall harmony of your face and jaws, and she listens carefully to your concerns and goals. If orthodontic treatment is warranted, she schedules a records appointment to capture digital X-rays, photographs, and a three-dimensional scan of your dental arches. These detailed records are the foundation upon which she builds a personalised treatment plan.

The treatment plan is presented to you in a thorough, unhurried discussion. she explains the technique she recommends, why she believes it is the best fit for your case, how long treatment should take, and what it will cost. she encourages questions and she wants you to feel genuinely comfortable before moving ahead. With Clichy only ten minutes away, even these preliminary appointments are easy to accommodate in your calendar.

Active treatment runs for twelve to twenty-four months in most cases. You will visit the practice every 6 to 12 weeks for adjustments or to pick up new aligners. Each visit is efficient and focused. When active treatment concludes, the retention phase begins. she fits bonded retainers behind the front teeth and may also provide removable retainers to be worn at night. Retention is the safeguard that ensures your orthodontic results remain stable over the long term, and she takes it very seriously.
