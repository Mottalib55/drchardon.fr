How to reach the practice from Saint-Cloud

By car, the journey from Saint-Cloud to the Colombes practice takes approximately twelve minutes under normal traffic conditions. The most direct route follows the N13 towards La Defense, then turns northward through Courbevoie to reach Colombes. An alternative uses the A86 from Saint-Cloud heading north-west, exiting at Colombes or Nanterre. Parking is available in the residential streets around the practice and is considerably easier than in central Saint-Cloud.

By public transport, the journey takes approximately twenty-five minutes. The most comfortable option is the T2 tramway from Val de Seine or Parc de Saint-Cloud station towards La Defense, then the Transilien L towards Colombes. The Gare du Stade is directly served by this line. From Saint-Cloud station on the Transilien L (Saint-Nom-la-Breteche branch), it is also possible to reach La Defense then change for à Colombes-bound train.

By bicycle, the route takes around thirty minutes following the banks of the Seine through Suresnes and Puteaux, then crossing to Courbevoie and Colombes. The terrain is relatively flat once the hillsides of Saint-Cloud have been descended, and cycle paths along the Seine make it a safe and pleasant journey.

Saint-Cloud and its residents

Saint-Cloud is a town of approximately 30,000 inhabitants, nestled on the hillsides overlooking the bend of the Seine to the west of Paris. The town is renowned for its Parc de Saint-Cloud, a 460-hectare national estate that was once the setting for an imperial chateau destroyed during the Franco-Prussian War of 1870. This park, with its formal French gardens, monumental fountains, grand vistas and woodlands, constitutes an exceptional green space and gives the town a privileged living environment.

Saint-Cloud is a residential town known for its quality of life. Ranked among the most affluent communes in the Hauts-de-Seine, it attracts families seeking a calm, leafy environment just minutes from Paris and La Defense. The Grande Rue, the main shopping street, is lined with boutiques, restaurants and local shops. The market, held several times a week, is a gathering place for residents attached to local quality of life.

The town is organised into several districts with distinct atmospheres. The town centre concentrates shops and services. Montretout, on the heights, offers panoramic views of Paris. Val d'Or is a residential area valued for its tranquillity. The Coteaux, climbing towards Garches and Marnes-la-Coquette, features detached housing in a heavily wooded setting. Saint-Cloud also boasts reputable schools that attract families who value their children's education.

The residents of Saint-Cloud whom Dr Chardon sees at the practice are often executives, professionals or families long established in the town. They share a common expectation of quality care and seek a practitioner capable of offering modern, discréét and effective orthodontic solutions.

Orthodontic treatments for patients from Saint-Cloud

Clear aligners are the most popular treatment among patients from Saint-Cloud. In a town where senior executives and professionals are numérous, discretion is a determining criterion. The transparent trays are virtually imperceptible when worn, allowing patients to continue their professional and social life unnoticed. The aligners are removed for meals and toothbrushing. The 3D digital planning Dr Chardon uses allows patients to visualise the expected result before starting. Follow-up appointments are spaced 6 to 12 weeks apart.

Multi-bracket braces remain essential for complex orthodontic cases. When severe crowding, significant malposition or jaw discrepancies require precise and powerful tooth movements, brackets bonded to each tooth offer unmatched control. She offers both classic metal and transparent ceramic brackets. Teenâgers from Saint-Cloud are often treated with multi-bracket braces when corrections are substantial.

A narrow palate, a receding or protruding lower jaw, permanent teeth that cannot find room to emerge: these situations are corrected more easily during the growth period. Interceptive orthodontics can considerably simplify, or even avoid, comprehensive treatment during adolescence.

Adult orthodontics représents a significant proportion of her activity. Adults from Saint-Cloud consult for various reasons: correcting an aesthetic defect, preparing teeth before prosthetic or implant work, or addressing teeth that have shifted after previous treatment. Current techniques treat adults with the same effectiveness as teenâgers, taking into account existing crowns, gum recession, or missing teeth.

Treatment process for a patient from Saint-Cloud

The first step is an assessment consultation. Dr Chardon examines the patient's teeth, takes photographs, and discusses expectations and concerns. This consultation lasts approximately thirty minutes. If orthodontic treatment is viable, she prescribes a panoramic radiograph, a lateral cephalometric radiograph and 3D digital impressions taken at the practice.

She then draws up a personalised treatment plan détailing the recommended technique, estimated duration, appointment frequency, total cost and insurance coverâge options. She présents this plan with visual simulations of the expected result. The patient has all the time needed to reflect before committing.

During the active treatment phase, check-up appointments are scheduled every 6 to 12 weeks. For a patient from Saint-Cloud, this means a trip roughly every month and a half to three months, which is very reasonable given the twelve-minute drive or twenty-five-minute public transport journey. Each session allows her to verify progress, adjust appliances, and answer all questions.

At the end of treatment, the retention phase ensures stability of results. A bonded retainer wire behind the front teeth maintains alignment. A night-time retainer may complement this. Retention check-ups are scheduled at regular intervals during the first two years, then less frequently. This rigour in post-treatment follow-up makes the difference between a lasting result and one that deteriorates over time.
