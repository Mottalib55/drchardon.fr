Orthodontic treatments available

As a dental surgeon whose practice is limited to orthodontics, she offers the full range of orthodontic treatments at her Colombes practice. Every treatment plan begins with a thorough initial consultation, during which she carries out a clinical examination, take digital photographs, and order any necessary imaging. Only after analysing all of this information do she proposes a personalised treatment approach.

Clear aligners are among the most requested treatments, particularly by adults and older teenagers from the 17th arrondissement. These removable, transparent trays gradually reposition the teeth over a series of stages. They are virtually invisible when worn, which makes them popular with professionals who do not want their treatment to be noticeable in meetings or social settings. Aligners also make oral hygiene simpler, since you remove them to eat and brush.

Multi-bracket braces remain the reference technique for many orthodontic corrections, especially complex cases involving significant crowding, deep bites, or jaw discrepancies. Modern brackets are smaller and more comfortable than those of previous generations. she uses both metal and ceramic brackets depending on the clinical situation and the patient's aesthetic preferences.

Interceptive orthodontics is aimed at younger children, typically between the ages of 6 and 10. At this stage, the jaw is still growing, and targeted interventions -- such as palatal expanders or partial braces -- can prevent more serious problems from developing later. she recommends that children have their first orthodontic assessment around age 7, even if treatment is not immediately needed. Many families from the 17th bring their children for a screening visit, and in a good number of cases, the conclusion is simply to monitor and wait.

Teenage orthodontics covers the period when most of the permanent teeth have erupted, usually between ages 11 and 16. This is the classic window for comprehensive orthodontic treatment. Whether we use braces or aligners depends on the complexity of the case and the teenager's own preferences and maturity.

Adult orthodontics has grown significantly in recent years. There is no upper age limit for orthodontic treatment, provided the teeth and supporting bone are healthy. Many adults come to her having lived with a misalignment they have been self-conscious about for years. Others are referred by their dentist because correcting the bite will help preserve teeth that are wearing unevenly or are difficult to clean properly.

Treatment process

The journey begins with an initial consultation. During this appointment, she examines your teeth, jaws, and facial profile. she takes the time to listen to what concerns you -- whether it is the appearance of your smile, difficulty chewing, or a recommendation from your dentist. Based on this first assessment, she will explain whether orthodontic treatment is indicated and outline the broad options available.

If we decide to proceed, the next step involves diagnostic records: digital impressions or moulds, panoramic and cephalometric X-rays, and intraoral photographs. These records allow her to study your case in detail and design a treatment plan tailored to your specific anatomy and goals. she thens present this plan to you at a second appointment, explaining the proposed mechanics, the estimated duration (which typically ranges from 12 to 24 months for comprehensive treatment), and the costs involved.

Once treatment begins, you will come to the practice for adjustment or monitoring appointments every 6 to 12 weeks. The exact frequency depends on the type of appliance and the stage of treatment. Each visit is relatively brief -- often 20 to 30 minutes -- and she will update you on progress at every appointment. Living in the 17th arrondissement means that these regular visits require minimal disruption to your day; you can easily schedule an appointment before work, during a lunch break, or on your way home.

After the active phase of treatment is complete, retention is essential. she will fit a bonded retainer behind your front teeth and provide removable retainer trays to wear at night. Retention is what ensures your results are stable over time, and she monitors it during periodic follow-up visits.
