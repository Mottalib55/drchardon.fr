Orthodontics in Le Port-Marly

Dr Chardon's practice in Colombes is accessible in 18 minutes by car from Le Port-Marly.

Your dental surgeon in Colombes, near Le Port-Marly

Dr Sarah Chardon's practice is located in Colombes, approximately eighteen minutes by car from Le Port-Marly. She is a dental surgeon whose practice is limited to orthodontics. Le Port-Marly is a small commune in the Yvelines, nestled between Marly-le-Roi and the banks of the Seine. This quiet residential town offers families a pleasant living environment with easy access to Colombes for specialist orthodontic care.

Her practice is exclusively devoted to orthodontics, ensuring patients from Le Port-Marly benefit from expert care and the most current techniques. Check-up appointments are spaced six to twelve weeks apart.

How to reach the practice from Le Port-Marly

By car, the journey takes approximately eighteen minutes via the D113 towards Chatou and then the A86 towards Colombes. By public transport, the journey takes approximately thirty minutes via the RER A from Marly-le-Roi or Le Pecq to La Defense, then the Transilien L to Colombes.

Le Port-Marly and its residents

Le Port-Marly is a commune of approximately 5,000 inhabitants. Historically linked to the Machine de Marly, which once pumped water from the Seine to supply the fountains of Versailles, the town retains a rich heritage. The population is predominantly family-oriented, with residents who appreciate the peaceful, leafy setting while remaining well-connected to the western Paris suburbs.

Orthodontic treatments for patients from Le Port-Marly

Dr Chardon offers the full range of orthodontic treatments: clear aligners for discreet adult treatment, multi-bracket braces for complex cases, interceptive orthodontics for young children, and adult orthodontics for functional and aesthetic corrections. Each treatment is personalised using digital planning.

Treatment process and why choose the Colombes practice

An in-depth initial consultation is followed by a personalised treatment plan. Active treatment involves check-ups every 6 to 12 weeks, and retention ensures lasting results. Dr Chardon's exclusive orthodontic specialisation and the practice's accessibility from Le Port-Marly make it a strong choice for residents seeking expert care.
