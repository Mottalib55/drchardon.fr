Epinay-sur-Seine and its residents

Epinay-sur-Seine sits on the right bank of the Seine in the northern part of the Seine-Saint-Denis department, bordered by Villetaneuse to the east, Saint-Denis to the south, and Ile-Saint-Denis and Villeneuve-la-Garenne to the west. With a population of approximately 56,000, it is one of the more densely populated communes in the area.

The town has a complex social fabric. The Orgemont neighbourhood in the south is dominated by large housing estates and is undergoing a significant urban renewal programme. The centre-ville around the Mairie and the Place du Marche retains a more traditional small-town feel, with local shops, cafes, and the weekly market. The northern sector, towards the Enghien-les-Bains border, is quieter and more residential, with detached houses and small apartment blocks along streets like the Rue de Paris and the Avenue de la Republique.

Families make up a large proportion of the population, and the town has numerous primary schools, colleges, and the Lycee Jacques-Feyder. This means there is significant local demand for both interceptive orthodontics for younger children and comprehensive treatment for teenagers. I also see a growing number of adult patients from Epinay who are choosing to address alignment issues later in life, often prompted by concerns about tooth wear, gum health, or simply wanting to feel more confident about their smile.

The multicultural character of Epinay-sur-Seine is reflected in the diversity of my patient base from the town. I make a point of ensuring that every patient, regardless of background, feels welcome and fully understood during their time at the practice.

Orthodontic treatments for patients from Epinay-sur-Seine

My practice in Colombes provides the full range of orthodontic treatments, each tailored to the specific clinical needs and personal preferences of the patient. For residents of Epinay-sur-Seine, here is what I offer.

Early treatment for children. I follow the recommended guideline of seeing children for their first orthodontic evaluation around age 6 to 7. At this age, I can detect developing issues such as crossbites, open bites, and severe crowding before they become more difficult and costly to treat. Interceptive appliances, which may be removable or fixed, work with the child's natural growth to correct or minimise these problems. Parents in Epinay-sur-Seine often appreciate the reassurance that comes from knowing their child's dental development is being monitored by a specialist.

Fixed braces for adolescents. When a teenager needs comprehensive orthodontic treatment, fixed multi-bracket braces are frequently the most effective approach. I offer both traditional metal brackets and tooth-coloured ceramic brackets, depending on the patient's preference. My systems use self-ligating technology, which means lighter forces, less friction, and typically fewer emergency visits. A standard course of treatment runs between 12 and 24 months, with adjustments every 6 to 12 weeks.

Clear aligner treatment. Transparent removable aligners are increasingly popular with both adult and teenage patients from Epinay-sur-Seine. These custom-made trays move teeth gradually over a planned sequence of stages. They can be removed for meals and oral hygiene, and they are almost invisible when worn. I use digital treatment simulation so that you can see a preview of your expected results before committing to treatment.

Comprehensive adult treatment. Adults considering orthodontics for the first time often have questions about whether it is too late, how visible the appliance will be, and how treatment fits around a professional life. I address all of these during the initial consultation. The short answer is that it is never too late, and modern techniques make treatment far more discreet and comfortable than many adults expect. Whether you choose fixed braces or aligners, I design your treatment plan to minimise disruption and maximise results.

Treatment process for a patient from Epinay-sur-Seine

The treatment journey at my practice follows a carefully structured pathway that begins well before any appliance is placed.

During the initial consultation, which lasts around 45 minutes, I examine your teeth, gums, and jaw joints in detail. I take a digital scan of your dental arches, clinical photographs, and request any radiographs that are needed for a complete diagnosis. We then have an open conversation about your concerns, your expectations, and the options that might be suitable for your case.

I take the diagnostic records away and spend time conducting a thorough analysis, measuring tooth positions, evaluating bone support, and planning the mechanics of treatment. The result is a detailed, personalised treatment plan that I present to you at a second appointment. This document covers the recommended treatment approach, the estimated timeline, the total fee, and the available payment arrangements, including monthly instalments.

Once you give the go-ahead, we move to the active treatment phase. For fixed braces, the first appointment involves bonding brackets and placing the initial archwire. For aligner therapy, you receive your first sets of trays and I walk you through the daily routine of wearing and caring for them. Progress check-ups happen every 6 to 12 weeks, and each visit is typically brief, lasting 15 to 30 minutes depending on what adjustments are needed.

When your teeth have reached their target positions, I remove the active appliance and fit a retention device. This usually consists of a bonded wire behind the lower front teeth and sometimes behind the upper front teeth as well, combined with a removable retainer for night-time wear. Retention is crucial, because teeth have a natural tendency to drift back toward their original positions if they are not held in place. I schedule periodic retention check-ups to ensure long-term stability.
