The 6th arrondissement and its residents

The 6th arrondissement occupies a singular place in the cultural imagination of Paris and of France as a whole. Saint-Germain-des-Pres, its emblematic neighbourhood, was the epicentre of intellectual and artistic life in the post-war period. The Cafe de Flore, Les Deux Magots and the Brasserie Lipp were the regular haunts of Sartre, Beauvoir, Camus and a constellation of writers, philosophers and artists who shaped 20th-century thought. Today, these establishments continue to attract a clientele that values culture, conversation and refinement.

The Jardin du Luxembourg, created by Marie de Medicis in 1612, is the crown jewel of the arrondissement. This 23-hectare garden, with its formal French parterre, English landscape garden, orchard, ponds and iconic green chairs, is one of the most beloved public spaces in Paris. Families bring their children to sail model boats on the central basin, joggers circle the gravel paths, and chess players gather under the chestnut trees. The Palais du Luxembourg, which houses the French Senate, presides over the northern end of the gardens.

The 6th is also home to some of Paris's most prestigious publishing houses, clustered around the Rue de l'Odeon and the Rue de Seine. The Ecole des Beaux-Arts, the Institut de France (which houses the Academie francaise) and numerous art galleries reinforce the arrondissement's intellectual and artistic identity. The Rue de Rennes, the Boulevard Raspail and the Rue du Cherche-Midi offer upscale shopping alongside artisan bakeries and gourmet food shops.

The population of the 6th is predominantly composed of established professionals, families from the upper-middle class, and long-standing residents who are deeply attached to their neighbourhood. This discerning population expects the highest standard of healthcare. Many of her patients from the 6th arrondissement are professionals or parents who choose orthodontic treatment for themselves or their children after careful research, and who appreciate a practitioner who combines expertise, transparency and a personalised approach.

Orthodontic treatments offered to patients from the 6th

Dr Chardon's practice is exclusively focused on orthodontics, ensuring the highest level of clinical expertise. Clear aligners are the treatment most frequently chosen by patients from the 6th arrondissement. These custom-made transparent trays offer a virtually invisible way to correct dental alignment issues, and they can be removed for meals and social events. For the professionals and public-facing individuals of Saint-Germain-des-Pres, aligners represent the ideal combination of efficacy and discretion. Treatment duration typically ranges from 6 to 18 months, depending on the complexity of the case.

Multi-bracket braces are indicated for complex malocclusions, significant jaw discrepancies and cases requiring precise mechanical control. Dr Chardon offers ceramic brackets that blend discreetly with the tooth surface, as well as traditional metal brackets when maximum strength is required. For teenagers in the 6th, many of whom attend prestigious lycees, she takes care to explain the treatment in a way that fosters understanding and cooperation, and she offers ceramic options to address aesthetic concerns.

Interceptive orthodontics is designed for children between the ages of 6 and 11. A first assessment at this age can identify jaw growth anomalies, dental crowding or harmful habits that, if addressed early, can be corrected with a short, targeted treatment of 6 to 12 months. This can prevent the need for more extensive treatment during adolescence. Families in the 6th, who are typically well-informed and proactive about their children's health, are among the most receptive to this early-intervention approach.

For adults with skeletal discrepancies requiring surgical correction, Dr Chardon works in collaboration with maxillofacial surgeons to plan and execute combined orthodontic-surgical treatments. Her exclusive focus on orthodontics ensures optimal preparation for the surgical phase.

Treatment process for a patient from the 6th arrondissement

The care pathway starts with a comprehensive initial consultation. Dr Chardon performs a thorough clinical examination, takes digital photographs and X-rays, and creates precise digital impressions using an intraoral scanner. She then presents a detailed treatment plan, explaining each option, the expected timeline, the number of appointments and the complete fees. Her approach is founded on transparency and trust: patients are never rushed into a decision and always leave with the information they need to choose with confidence.

Follow-up appointments are scheduled every 6 to 12 weeks throughout the active treatment phase. Each session is efficient and carefully timed, lasting 15 to 30 minutes. Dr Chardon verifies the progression of tooth movements, makes any necessary adjustments and monitors oral hygiene. For patients from the 6th, these spaced appointments mean visiting the Colombes practice only a few times per year, keeping disruption to a minimum.

The retention phase is an essential final step. A bonded lingual wire or a custom night retainer tray keeps the teeth in their corrected position. Regular checks during the first year after the end of active treatment ensure lasting stability. Dr Chardon is committed to results that endure over time, and her retention protocol reflects that commitment.
