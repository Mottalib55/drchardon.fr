Saint-Denis and its residents

Saint-Denis is, by any measure, one of the most important cities in the northern suburbs of Paris. The Confluence district, where the Canal Saint-Denis meets the Seine, is undergoing major redevelopment in connection with the 2024 Olympic legacy.
