Orthodontic treatments for patients from La Garenne-Colombes

I provide the complete spectrum of orthodontic treatments at my Colombes practice. For younger children, interceptive orthodontics addresses developing problems early, before all the permanent teeth have erupted. A crossbite, a narrow upper jaw, or severely protruding front teeth can often be corrected with a focused course of treatment lasting six to twelve months, potentially avoiding the need for more extensive intervention later.

For teenagers, multi-bracket braces remain the workhorse of orthodontic treatment. I use modern self-ligating bracket systems that apply gentle, continuous forces to the teeth, resulting in more efficient movement and, in many cases, shorter treatment times. Ceramic brackets, which are tooth-coloured and far less visible than traditional metal, are available for patients who are self-conscious about their appearance during treatment.

Clear aligners have become the treatment of choice for many adults from La Garenne-Colombes. These custom-fabricated, transparent trays are virtually invisible when worn and can be removed for eating and brushing. Using digital scans and specialised software, I design the full sequence of aligners at the outset, and patients can preview their projected outcome on screen before committing to treatment. That said, aligners are not the right solution for every malocclusion. In cases involving significant skeletal discrepancy or complex tooth movements, braces may produce a better result, and I always recommend the approach that I believe will deliver the most reliable, long-lasting outcome.

Treatment process for a patient from La Garenne-Colombes

Everything begins with a first consultation. I examine your teeth, jaw, and bite, listen to your concerns, and discuss your goals. If treatment is indicated, we schedule a records appointment at which I take detailed digital photographs, panoramic and cephalometric X-rays, and a three-dimensional scan of your dental arches. These records form the foundation of a tailored treatment plan.

When the plan is ready, I walk you through it step by step. I explain which technique I recommend and why, how long treatment is expected to take, and what the costs will be. I want you to feel fully informed and genuinely comfortable with the proposed approach before we proceed. For patients from La Garenne-Colombes, the convenience of the practice location means that the fitting appointment -- whether bonding brackets or delivering the first set of aligners -- is never a burden to schedule.

During active treatment, which typically spans twelve to twenty-four months, you will visit me every 6 to 12 weeks for adjustments or to collect new aligners. At the five-minute distance between La Garenne-Colombes and Colombes, these appointments are quick and easy to integrate into your routine. Once the active phase is complete, retention begins. I normally place a thin bonded wire behind the front teeth and, depending on the case, provide a removable night retainer as well. Retention is non-negotiable: it is what ensures your results stay in place for years to come.
