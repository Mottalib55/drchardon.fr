Villetaneuse and its residents

Villetaneuse is a small commune of approximately 13,000 inhabitants, tucked between Epinay-sur-Seine to the west, Pierrefitte-sur-Seine to the north, Stains to the east, and Saint-Denis to the south. Despite its modest size, Villetaneuse punches above its weight in several respects, largely thanks to the presence of the Universite Sorbonne Paris Nord (formerly Paris 13), whose main campus dominates the southern part of the town.

The university campus is a defining feature of the commune. It brings thousands of students into Villetaneuse daily, along with faculty and administrative staff, creating a dynamic atmosphere that contrasts with the quieter residential streets in the northern part of town. The student population adds a youthful energy and also generates a specific demand for orthodontic care: many young adults at university realise it is a good time to address alignment issues, either through clear aligners that fit around their academic schedule or through traditional braces.

The residential areas of Villetaneuse are concentrated along the Rue de la Republique, the Avenue de la Division-Leclerc, and the streets radiating from the town centre. Housing ranges from small apartment buildings to semi-detached houses, and the town maintains several green spaces including the Parc de la Butte Pinson on the northern boundary, which offers pleasant walking trails and views over the surrounding urban landscape.

The population includes many families with children, and the town's primary schools and the College Jean-Vilar generate referrals for paediatric and adolescent orthodontic evaluations. she sees patients from across the age spectrum in Villetaneuse: children whose developing dentition needs monitoring, teenagers with crowding or bite problems, university students who want to straighten their teeth before entering the workforce, and established adults who have decided it is time to address a long-standing concern.

Orthodontic treatments for patients from Villetaneuse

Her Colombes practice offers the full range of orthodontic treatments. she nevers apply a cookie-cutter approach; every treatment plan is tailored to the patient's specific clinical situation, aesthetic preferences, and lifestyle. For residents of Villetaneuse, here is what she provides.

Interceptive orthodontics for children. A first orthodontic evaluation around age 6 or 7 can reveal problems that are easier to treat early: crossbites, protruding front teeth, severe crowding, or narrow upper jaws. Using targeted appliances such as palatal expanders, space maintainers, or removable functional devices, she can guide growth and prevent minor issues from becoming major ones. For parents in Villetaneuse, this early intervention often means simpler, shorter, and less costly treatment when the child reaches their teens.

Fixed braces for adolescents and adults. Multi-bracket fixed appliances are the workhorse of orthodontics for good reason: they are incredibly versatile and can treat virtually any type of malocclusion. she uses self-ligating systems, available in both metallic and ceramic versions, which apply lighter forces, create less friction, and often reduce the number of appointments needed. For teenagers from Villetaneuse, treatment typically runs 12 to 24 months. For adults, it may be slightly longer depending on the complexity of the case and the response of the supporting bone.

Clear aligner therapy. Removable transparent aligners have become a mainstream treatment option, and she sees strong demand for them from both university students and working adults in Villetaneuse. Each aligner is custom-manufactured from a digital plan, and the sequence of trays guides teeth progressively into alignment. Treatment planning includes a digital simulation that allows you to preview the expected results. Aligners are removed for meals and oral hygiene, and they are virtually invisible to others, making them an appealing choice for anyone who values discretion.

Comprehensive adult orthodontics. Whether you are a postgraduate student at Sorbonne Paris Nord or a long-time Villetaneuse resident in your 40s or 50s, it is never too late to pursue orthodontic treatment. she helps adult patients address crowding, spacing, overbites, underbites, and crossbites using whichever technique is best suited to their anatomy and objectives. Modern materials and methods have made adult treatment more comfortable and less conspicuous than many people expect.

Treatment process for a patient from Villetaneuse

The treatment pathway at her practice is carefully structured to ensure that every decision is informed and every step is transparent.

Everything begins with the initial consultation, a 45-minute appointment during which she carries out a comprehensive examination. she assesses your teeth, your bite, your jaw joints, and your facial proportions. she captures a digital scan of your dental arches, take photographs, and order any radiographs that are necessary. We then sit together and discuss what you are hoping to achieve, what concerns you about your current dental situation, and any previous orthodontic or dental history she shoulds be aware of.

Following this appointment, she spends time performing a detailed diagnostic analysis. she studies the scans, measure cephalometric angles and tooth dimensions, evaluate periodontal health and root morphology, and design the mechanics of the proposed treatment. The output is a written treatment plan that covers the recommended approach, the expected timeline, a complete cost breakdown, and the available payment options including monthly instalment plans. she presents this at a second appointment and give you full space to ask questions and consider your options.

When you are ready, we begin active treatment. For fixed braces, the first session involves bonding brackets and placing the initial wire. For aligner therapy, you receive your first trays along with thorough instructions on how to use and maintain them. From this point, you return to the practice every 6 to 12 weeks for progress checks and adjustments. Each visit is typically 15 to 30 minutes long and is designed to be focused and efficient.

At the end of the active phase, she fits a retainer to keep your teeth in their corrected positions. she uses bonded wires behind the front teeth, sometimes supplemented by removable night-time retainers. Retention is not optional; it is an essential part of the treatment that ensures your investment of time and money produces lasting results. she monitors retention with periodic check-ups and make adjustments if needed.
