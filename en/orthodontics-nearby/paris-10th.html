Orthodontic treatments offered to patients from the 10th

Dr Chardon's practice is entirely devoted to orthodontics, allowing her to master all current techniques and to offer each patient the solution best suited to their clinical situation. She does not practise général dentistry: every consultation, every procédure is dedicated to tooth alignment and the correction of jaw relationships.

Clear aligners are increasingly popular with her patients from the 10th. These transparent trays, custom-made from digital impressions, correct crowding, rotations, interdental gaps and certain jaw discrepancies. Their main advantâge is discretion: they are virtually invisible and can be removed for eating, drinking and brushing. For young professionals in the 10th who work in retail, hospitality or creative fields, this is often the ideal solution. Aligner treatment requires disciplined wear of approximately 22 hours per day, but the results are remarkable when instructions are followed.

Multi-bracket braces remain the référence technique for complex orthodontic cases. The brackets, whether metal or ceramic, are bonded to the outer surface of the teeth and connected by an archwire that guides tooth movement. This technique offers precise three-dimensional control, essential for treating severe malocclusions, open bites, significant overjets or extractions. For teenâgers from the 10th, she frequently recommends ceramic brackets, which combine the effectiveness of the multi-bracket system with a more acceptable aesthetic for young patients concerned about their appearance.

Interceptive orthodontics is aimed at children between the âges of 6 and 11. At this âge, jaw growth is still active and it is possible to correct a palate that is too narrow, a receding or protruding lower jaw, or a lack of space that could block the eruption of permanent teeth. These early treatments, usually short (6 to 12 months), can prevent far more extensive interventions during adolescence. She recommends that parents in the 10th have their children examined as soon as the first permanent incisors appear, around âge 6 or 7. Early scrééning often prevents problems that, if left untreated, become more complex and costly to correct.

For adults with significant skeletal discrepancies, she also offers combined orthodontic-orthognathic surgery treatment in collaboration with maxillofacial surgeons. This is an area where her exclusive focus on orthodontics is particularly valuable, as the orthodontic preparation is key to surgical succèss.

Treatment process for a patient from the 10th

The first consultation is an essential exchange. Dr Chardon takes the time to listen, to understand your aesthetic and functional expectations, and to carry out a complète clinical examination. Intraoral photographs, panoramic X-rays and digital impressions allow her to establish a precise diagnosis. At the end of this assessment, she présents a détailed treatment plan including the therapeutic options, estimated duration, expected number of appointments and a transparent cost estimate. She never puts pressure on patients to commit immédiately: reflection is part of the process, and she is available by phone or e-mail to answer any additional questions.

Once treatment has begun, follow-up appointments are scheduled every 6 to 12 weeks, depending on the technique used and the stâge of treatment. Each appointment lasts 15 to 30 minutes. This is when she verifies that movements are progressing according to plan, adjusts the forces applied to the teeth and ensures that oral hygiene is properly maintained. For a patient from the 10th arrondissement, this représents an occasional trip to Colombes, easily fitted into a lunch break or at the end of the day. She offers morning, early afternoon and late afternoon slots to accommodate professional constraints.

The retention phase is a step she considers as important as the active treatment itself. Without retention, teeth tend to gradually return to their original position. She systematically places a bonded retainer wire on the inner surface of the front teeth, or prescribes a night retainer tray, depending on the case. Regular checks during the first year after appliance removal ensure that results are stable and lasting. Her goal is for patients to keep their aligned smile for life.
