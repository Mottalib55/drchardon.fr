Nanterre and its residents

Nanterre is one of the largest communes in the Hauts-de-Seine, both in surface area and population. Its roughly 95,000 inhabitants live across a diverse urban landscape that includes the modern towers and shopping complexes around the RER A corridor, the historic streets near the old centre and the Cathedrale Sainte-Genevieve-et-Saint-Maurice, the sprawling campus of Paris Nanterre University, and quieter residential neighbourhoods like the Quartier du Petit Nanterre and Mont-Valerien. The Seine forms the city's northern boundary, and the strategic location of Nanterre at the western gate of Paris has shaped its development for centuries.

The presence of the university, with its population of over 30,000 students, gives the city a youthful energy. Families are also well represented, with a strong network of schools at every level, from the ecoles maternelles through to the Lycee Joliot-Curie and beyond. Nanterre's cultural life benefits from venues such as the Maison de la Musique and the Theatre Nanterre-Amandiers, a nationally significant centre for contemporary theatre. The Parc Andre Malraux, a large green space near the prefecture, is a popular destination for families and joggers alike.

The size and diversity of Nanterre's population mean that her patient base from this city spans a wide spectrum. she sees young children referred for early orthodontic evaluation by their family dentist, teenagers at various stages of dental development, university students who want to take advantage of their time in the area to start treatment, and adults of all ages seeking to improve their dental alignment. Orthodontic treatments for patients from Nanterre

At her practice in Colombes, she offers every major category of orthodontic treatment. Interceptive orthodontics is designed for children between the ages of six and eleven, addressing problems like crossbites, underbites, narrow upper jaws, and severe crowding while the bones are still growing and adaptable. These early interventions typically last six to twelve months and can substantially reduce the scope of any treatment needed later.

For adolescents from Nanterre, multi-bracket braces are the most common treatment. she uses modern self-ligating bracket systems that deliver controlled, efficient tooth movement. Ceramic brackets that blend with the natural tooth colour are available for patients who are concerned about aesthetics during treatment. Clear aligners have become increasingly popular among adults in Nanterre, particularly professionals and university staff who prefer a treatment that is nearly invisible and compatible with their daily routines. These transparent, removable trays are planned digitally from the outset, and patients can see a visualisation of their expected results before beginning.

Choosing the right technique is one of the most important decisions in orthodontic treatment, and she alwayses base her recommendation on a thorough clinical analysis. If a patient asks for aligners but their case would be better treated with braces, she explains why. If a simpler approach will achieve the desired result, she does not recommend a more complex one. Honesty and clinical judgement are at the core of her practice.

Treatment process for a patient from Nanterre

The process begins with an initial consultation. she examines your teeth, bite, and jaw function, and she takes the time to listen to what you hope to achieve. If she believes orthodontic treatment is appropriate, the next step is a diagnostic records appointment where she captures digital X-rays, clinical photographs, and a three-dimensional scan of your teeth. These records allow her to build a detailed, accurate treatment plan.

she presents the plan to you in a dedicated appointment, covering the proposed technique, the expected duration, the costs, and any alternative approaches. she believes that informed patients make better decisions, and she alwayses encourage questions. For patients from Nanterre, the clarity and structure of this process is often cited as one of the things they appreciate most about her practice. Once you decide to proceed, the fitting appointment is scheduled without delay.

Active treatment usually lasts between twelve and twenty-four months, with follow-up visits every 6 to 12 weeks. Each visit is efficient, typically lasting twenty to forty minutes. With Nanterre twelve minutes away, these appointments are easy to manage alongside work, school, or university commitments. After active treatment ends, retention begins. she fits bonded retainers behind the front teeth and may also provide removable night retainers. she follows up during the retention period to make certain your results are holding, because a beautiful smile is only valuable if it lasts.
