Orthodontics Nearby Eastern Yvelines

Dr Chardon's practice in Colombes is 10 to 25 minutes from Sartrouville, Houilles, Chatou and the cities of eastern Yvelines.

Orthodontics in Colombes: a practice close to eastern Yvelines

Dr Sarah Chardon's orthodontic practice is located in Colombes (92700) and welcomes residents from across eastern Yvelines. The practice is served by the Transilien L line, which connects Colombes to many Yvelines stations, as well as by the D308 road and the A86 motorway.

Sartrouville and Houilles, the two nearest Yvelines cities, are only 10 to 12 minutes away by car. Carrieres-sur-Seine, Chatou, Le Vesinet and Montesson sit within the Seine loop and are 15 to 18 minutes away. Maisons-Laffitte, Rueil-Malmaison, Saint-Germain-en-Laye and Poissy can be reached in 20 to 30 minutes.

Getting to the practice from the Yvelines

From Sartrouville and Houilles

Sartrouville and Houilles are the closest Yvelines cities to the practice. The Transilien L line directly serves both stations as well as the Gare du Stade in Colombes, making the journey by public transport straightforward. By car, from Sartrouville take the D308 southward to reach Colombes in about 12 minutes. Houilles is even closer, around 10 minutes via the Boulevard Jean Jaures.

From Carrieres-sur-Seine, Chatou and Le Vesinet

These residential cities within the Seine loop are popular for their quality of life. From Chatou, take the D186 and cross the Seine via the Pont de Chatou to reach Colombes in roughly 15 minutes. Le Vesinet is about 18 minutes away along the same route. Carrieres-sur-Seine, on the right bank of the Seine, is reachable in 15 minutes through Houilles. The RER A serves Chatou and Le Vesinet, with connections available to reach Colombes.

From Maisons-Laffitte and Montesson

Maisons-Laffitte, known for its chateau and racecourse, is roughly 18 minutes away by car via the D308. The Transilien L line also serves the Maisons-Laffitte station. Montesson, a residential city between Sartrouville and Le Vesinet, connects to Colombes in about 15 minutes via local roads.

From Rueil-Malmaison, Saint-Germain-en-Laye and Poissy

Rueil-Malmaison is reachable in about 15 minutes via the A86 or the D913. Saint-Germain-en-Laye, with its famous terrace overlooking the Seine, is around 25 minutes away via the A13 or the RER A. Poissy, at the end of the line, takes approximately 25 minutes via the A13 or the D190 along the river.
