Orthodontic treatments for patients from Courbevoie

she offers the full spectrum of orthodontic care at her Colombes practice. Interceptive orthodontics for children aged six to eleven addresses early-stage problems such as crossbites, severe crowding, and protruding upper incisors. These targeted interventions, which usually last between six and twelve months, can significantly reduce the complexity of later treatment.

For teenagers, multi-bracket braces provide the most versatile and powerful approach to complex tooth movements. she uses self-ligating brackets that deliver gentle, consistent forces, and she offers ceramic brackets for patients who prefer a less conspicuous appearance. Clear aligners are increasingly popular among adults in Courbevoie, particularly professionals working at La Defense who appreciate a treatment that is virtually invisible during business meetings and client interactions. The aligners are removable, so there are no dietary limitations and cleaning remains easy.

The choice of technique is always based on a thorough diagnosis. she does not recommend aligners where braces would produce a better outcome, and she is frank about the limitations of each approach. Her goal is to provide each patient with the treatment that will deliver the best result in the most efficient way.

Treatment process for a patient from Courbevoie

Everything starts with a comprehensive initial consultation. she examines your teeth, your bite, and your jaw function, and we have an open conversation about what you would like to achieve. If treatment is indicated, she takes detailed records at a follow-up appointment: digital radiographs, photographs, and a 3D intra-oral scan. Using these records, she develops a treatment plan that is specific to your anatomy and your goals.

she thens present the plan to you in a dedicated discussion, explaining the proposed mechanics, the expected duration, the costs, and the alternatives. she wants every patient from Courbevoie to feel confident and well informed before making a decision. Once you decide to proceed, the fitting appointment is scheduled. For braces, this involves bonding the brackets to the teeth; for aligners, it means delivering the first sets of trays along with detailed instructions for wear.

During active treatment, you will visit the practice every 6 to 12 weeks. Each visit is typically short -- between twenty and forty minutes -- and with the eight-minute drive from Courbevoie, the round trip barely dents your day. Treatment duration ranges from twelve to twenty-four months in most cases. Upon completion, retention is essential. she fits a permanent bonded retainer behind the front teeth and, where indicated, supply a removable night retainer. she follows up during the retention period to ensure your teeth remain in their corrected positions.
