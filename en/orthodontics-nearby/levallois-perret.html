Orthodontic treatments for patients from Levallois-Perret

My practice offers the complete range of orthodontic treatments. Interceptive orthodontics for children aged six to eleven addresses early problems like crossbites, open bites, and severe crowding. By intervening during the mixed-dentition phase, I can often improve jaw growth and create better conditions for the permanent teeth to come through, reducing the complexity of any future treatment.

Multi-bracket braces are the treatment of choice for most teenage patients. I use self-ligating systems that produce efficient tooth movement with less discomfort. Ceramic brackets, which are nearly invisible against the teeth, are popular among image-conscious adolescents. For adults from Levallois-Perret, clear aligners are frequently preferred. These custom-fabricated, transparent trays are removable and virtually undetectable during wear. I plan each aligner case digitally, using 3D scans and specialised software, so patients can visualise their projected results before treatment starts.

The decision about which technique to use is always based on clinical factors. I assess each case individually and recommend the approach that will deliver the best outcome. If braces are more appropriate than aligners for a particular situation, I say so clearly and explain why. My patients appreciate that honesty.

Treatment process for a patient from Levallois-Perret

Treatment starts with a comprehensive initial consultation. I examine your teeth, bite, and jaw function, and we discuss your objectives. If orthodontic treatment is advisable, a records appointment follows, during which I capture digital X-rays, photographs, and a 3D intra-oral scan. From these records, I develop a treatment plan that is precisely tailored to your anatomy and your goals.

The plan is then presented in a detailed consultation where I walk you through every aspect: the chosen technique, the expected timeline, the costs, and any relevant alternatives. I want patients from Levallois-Perret to feel informed and empowered before making a decision. Once you choose to proceed, the fitting appointment is booked, and treatment begins.

During active treatment, which typically lasts twelve to twenty-four months, visits are scheduled every 6 to 12 weeks. Each appointment is efficient and focused. The twelve-minute journey from Levallois-Perret ensures that these visits do not become a logistical challenge. After the active phase, retention secures your results. I use bonded retainers behind the front teeth and may also prescribe a removable retainer for nighttime wear. Careful monitoring during retention is a non-negotiable part of my protocol, because maintaining results is just as important as achieving them.
