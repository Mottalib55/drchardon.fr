Orthodontics Nearby Northern Hauts-de-Seine

Dr Chardon's practice in Colombes is ideally located for residents of northern Hauts-de-Seine. Here is how to reach us from your area.

An orthodontic practice in Colombes, at the heart of northern Hauts-de-Seine

Dr Sarah Chardon is a dental surgeon whose practice is limited to orthodontics. The office is located in Colombes (92700), close to the Gare du Stade station (Transilien L). The practice welcomes residents from across the northern part of the Hauts-de-Seine department.

From Asnieres-sur-Seine, Bois-Colombes, La Garenne-Colombes, Courbevoie, Gennevilliers, Clichy, Levallois-Perret, Neuilly-sur-Seine, Puteaux or Nanterre, the practice can be reached within minutes by car or public transport.

Getting here from northern Hauts-de-Seine

From Asnieres-sur-Seine and Bois-Colombes

Asnieres and Bois-Colombes are the nearest cities to the practice. From Asnieres, take the Avenue d'Argenteuil or the Boulevard Voltaire to reach Colombes in about 5 minutes by car. From Bois-Colombes, the journey is even shorter -- around 3 minutes via the Rue des Bourguignons. By public transport, the Transilien L line connects Bois-Colombes station directly to the Gare du Stade in just a few stops.

From La Garenne-Colombes and Courbevoie

La Garenne-Colombes shares a direct border with Colombes. The drive takes roughly 5 minutes along the Boulevard de la Republique. Residents of Courbevoie can follow the Boulevard de Verdun and then the Avenue de l'Agent Sarre for a journey of about 8 minutes. The Transilien L line serves both cities.

From Gennevilliers and Villeneuve-la-Garenne

Gennevilliers is about 7 minutes away by car via the Boulevard Charles de Gaulle. Bus lines 235 and 304 also provide regular connections. From Villeneuve-la-Garenne, the trip takes approximately 10 minutes along the D986.

From Clichy, Levallois-Perret and Neuilly-sur-Seine

These three cities on the edge of Paris are well connected to Colombes. From Clichy, the journey takes around 10 minutes via the Pont de Clichy. Levallois-Perret and Neuilly can be reached in 12 to 15 minutes by car. The Transilien L from Gare Saint-Lazare runs directly to Colombes, which is convenient for residents of these Paris-adjacent cities.

From Puteaux, Nanterre and La Defense

The La Defense business district is only about 10 minutes from the practice by car. From Nanterre, the drive takes around 12 minutes via the A86 motorway or local roads. Bus lines and the Transilien also provide easy connections to the practice.
