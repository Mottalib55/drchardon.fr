Ville-d'Avray and its residents

Ville-d'Avray is a commune of approximately 11,000 inhabitants, set in a green haven between the Foret de Fausses-Reposes and the hillsides overlooking the Seine valley. The town is world-famous for its Etangs de Ville-d'Avray, immortalised by the painter Camille Corot, a major figure of the Barbizon school. These two ponds, set in a wooded valley of rare beauty, inspired dozens of canvases in the 19th century and continue to attract walkers, photographers and nature lovers.

The commune is bordered to the south and east by the Foret domaniale de Fausses-Reposes, a wooded area of nearly 600 hectares shared with Chaville, Marnes-la-Coquette and Versailles. This forest offers residents exceptional walking and leisure opportunities. The presence of this préservéd natural space contributes greatly to the very residential and leafy atmosphere of Ville-d'Avray.

Ville-d'Avray is a calm area, sought after by affluent families wanting a privileged living environment while remaining close to Paris, La Defense and Versailles. Noteworthy features include the Eglise Saint-Nicolas, a 17th-century church, and the Parc de la Ronce, a communal green space popular with families. The residents Dr Chardon sees at the practice reflect this distinctive commune: long-established families, executives and professionals, parents concerned about their children's dental health, and demanding adults.

The first step is an assessment consultation at the Colombes practice. Dr Chardon examines the patient's teeth, takes photographs, and discusses expectations, concerns and goals.
