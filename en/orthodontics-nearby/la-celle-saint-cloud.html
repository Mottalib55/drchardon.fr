How to reach the practice from La Celle-Saint-Cloud

By car, the journey takes approximately fifteen minutes via the D7 towards Bougival, then the A86 towards Colombes. An alternative route passes through Rueil-Malmaison. Parking around the practice is straightforward. By public transport, the journey takes approximately thirty minutes via the Transilien L from Bougival or La Defense to the Gare du Stade in Colombes.

La Celle-Saint-Cloud and its residents

La Celle-Saint-Cloud is a commune of approximately 22,000 inhabitants. The town is known for its Pavillon de Musique de la Comtesse du Barry, a fine 18th-century architectural gem. The residential neighbourhoods feature handsome villas surrounded by gardens, set against a backdrop of mature trees. The population is family-oriented and affluent, with many residents commuting to La Defense, Versailles or Paris. The town benefits from excellent schools and a strong local community.
