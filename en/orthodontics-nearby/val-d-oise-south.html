Orthodontics Nearby Southern Val-d'Oise

Dr Chardon's practice in Colombes is easily accessible from Argenteuil, Bezons and all the cities in southern Val-d'Oise.

An orthodontic practice within reach of southern Val-d'Oise

Dr Sarah Chardon's practice is located in Colombes (92700), right at the border between the Hauts-de-Seine and Val-d'Oise departments. The practice welcomes residents from the southern part of the Val-d'Oise (department 95), which is well connected to Colombes by road and public transport links running between the two departments.

The cities of Argenteuil and Bezons, separated from Colombes by the Seine, are reachable in roughly ten minutes. Further north, Sannois, Franconville, Cormeilles-en-Parisis, Ermont, Enghien-les-Bains, Saint-Gratien, Taverny, Herblay-sur-Seine and Montmorency all fall within a 20- to 25-minute drive.

Directions from southern Val-d'Oise

From Argenteuil and Bezons

Argenteuil is the largest nearby city on the Val-d'Oise side of the river. From the centre of Argenteuil, cross the Seine via the Pont d'Argenteuil (D311) and you will reach Colombes directly in about 10 minutes. By public transport, the Transilien J line connects Argenteuil to the Gare de Colombes. From Bezons, a similar route via the Pont de Bezons (D392) takes around 8 minutes by car.

From Sannois, Franconville and Cormeilles-en-Parisis

These three hillside cities in the Val-d'Oise are connected to Colombes via the D392 or the A15 motorway. From Sannois, the journey takes about 15 minutes through Argenteuil. Franconville is reachable in 18 minutes along the D928. Cormeilles-en-Parisis, perched above the Seine valley, connects to Colombes in 15 minutes via the D48 and the Pont de Bezons.

From Ermont, Enghien-les-Bains and Saint-Gratien

The Transilien H line serves Enghien-les-Bains and Ermont from the Gare du Nord, with connections available to Colombes via Saint-Lazare. By car, Ermont and Enghien are around 20 minutes away via the D928 or the A15. Saint-Gratien is a similar distance, reachable via the D311 through Argenteuil.

From Taverny, Herblay and Montmorency

These cities a little further north remain within a reasonable distance. Taverny is about 22 minutes away via the A15, and Herblay takes around 20 minutes via the D48. Montmorency, nestled in the valley of the same name, can be reached in roughly 25 minutes via Enghien and the A15.
