Orthodontic treatments offered to patients from Suresnes

Clear aligners are the most popular treatment among adult patients from Suresnes. These transparent, custom-made trays, manufactured using 3D digital planning, gradually correct dental alignment without the appliance being visible. For residents of Suresnes who work at La Defense or in demanding professional environments, this discretion is a decisive advantâge. The aligners are removed for meals and toothbrushing, which makes oral hygiene easier throughout the treatment. Check-up appointments are spaced eight to twelve weeks apart, limiting the number of trips to the practice. Before treatment begins, a digital simulation allows the patient to visualise the expected outcome, step by step, which serves as a powerful motivational tool.

Multi-bracket braces remain the gold standard for complex treatments. For teenâgers from Suresnes, multi-bracket braces are often the most appropriate choice when significant corrections are needed: severe dental crowding, impacted teeth, or jaw discrepancies. Metal brackets provide precise control over tooth movements and deliver reliable, long-lasting results. For patients concerned about aesthetics, transparent ceramic brackets offer an excellent alternative, combining performance and discretion. Results achieved with multi-bracket braces are générally faster than those with aligners in clinically challenging situations.

Interceptive orthodontics is for children âged six to nine. At this âge, the jaws are still actively growing, and it is possible to intervene to correct developmental abnormalities before they worsen. A palate that is too narrow, a receding lower jaw, chronic mouth breathing, or baby teeth that do not fall out at the right time are all signs that warrant an early consultation. Parents in Suresnes are often attentive to these signals and consult early, which allows intervention at the most favourable time. Interceptive treatment typically lasts a few months and can prevent more extensive treatment during adolescence.

Adult orthodontics représents a growing proportion of her activity. Many adult patients from Suresnes consult for aesthetic reasons -- a smile that has been a source of self-consciousness for years -- or for functional reasons: difficulty chewing, premature wear of certain teeth, or joint pain linked to poor dental occlusion. Modern techniques, whether aligners or multi-bracket braces, can effectively treat adults while taking into account the particularities of their dentition: the presence of crowns or bridges, gum recession, or the need to prepare for future implant work. The results often transform self-confidence and provide lasting improvement to oral health.
