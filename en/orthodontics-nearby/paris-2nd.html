Orthodontic treatments offered to patients from the 2nd

The practice offers the full range of current orthodontic treatments, adapted to each clinical situation. Clear aligners are the most requested treatment by patients from the 2nd arrondissement. These transparent trays, custom-made from digital impressions, correct dental alignment issues in a virtually invisible way. They can be removed for meals and brushing, making them particularly suited to the intense professional lives of residents in the Bourse and Sentier areas. Aligner treatment générally lasts between 6 and 18 months depending on the complexity of the case, with spaced check-ups at the practice.

Multi-bracket braces remain indicated for cases requiring precise and powerful three-dimensional control of tooth movements. Dr Chardon offers ceramic brackets that blend with the natural colour of the teeth, as well as traditional metal brackets for the most complex clinical situations. In teenâgers, braces often représent the best therapeutic option, as they can treat severe malocclusions while accommodating jaw growth.
