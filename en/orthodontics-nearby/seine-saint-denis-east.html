Orthodontics in Eastern Seine-Saint-Denis

Dr Chardon's practice in Colombes is accessible in 15 to 30 minutes from cities in the eastern part of Seine-Saint-Denis.

Beyond the neighbouring cities in western Seine-Saint-Denis (Saint-Denis, Saint-Ouen, Epinay), a second ring of cities further east in the department -- Aubervilliers, Pantin, Le Bourget, Drancy, Bobigny -- remains perfectly accessible from Dr Chardon's practice in Colombes. Connected by the A86, the A1 and Metro lines 5, 7 and 12 as well as the RER B, these cities allow a comfortable commute for regular orthodontic follow-up appointments.
