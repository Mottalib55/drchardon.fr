Clamart and its residents

Clamart is a commune of approximately 53,000 inhabitants in the southern Hauts-de-Seine. The town is known for its vast natural heritâge: the Foret domaniale de Meudon, often called the Bois de Clamart, extends over some 460 hectares at the edge of the commune and is one of the largest wooded areas in the inner Paris suburbs.
