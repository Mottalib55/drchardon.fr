Bougival and its residents

Bougival is a commune of approximately 8,500 inhabitants. The town's identity is deeply linked to the impressionist movement: the banks of the Seine here provided the subject for some of the most famous paintings of the 19th century. The Machine de Marly, an extraordinary hydraulic engineering feat built to supply the fountains of Versailles, was partly located in Bougival. Today, the town combines historical charm with modern residential comfort. The population includes families, retirees and professionals who appreciate the peaceful riverside setting and the proximity to La Defense.
