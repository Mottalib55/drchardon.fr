How to reach the practice from Chaville

By car, from central Chaville, the journey is smooth and direct. The fastest route joins the D910 towards Sevres, then takes the N118 towards the Pont de Sevres. From there, head towards La Defense via the Seine quays or the A86 north, before turning towards Colombes. This route takes approximately fifteen minutes under normal conditions. An alternative passes through Ville-d'Avray and Saint-Cloud to join the N13, then the A86 north. Parking is available in the residential streets around the practice.

By public transport, several options are available. The Chaville-Rive Droite station, served by the Transilien L, reaches La Defense (Grande Arche) in about ten minutes. From La Defense, the same Transilien L provides a direct link to the Gare du Stade in Colombes. The total door-to-door journey takes approximately thirty minutes. The Chaville-Velizy station on the Transilien N offers an alternative towards Paris-Montparnasse.

By bicycle, the journey from Chaville takes around forty-five minutes. The route passes through Sevres and follows the Seine towards Boulogne then Colombes. The terrain is hilly at the start but becomes flat once the quays are reached.

Chaville and its residents

Chaville is a commune of approximately 20,500 inhabitants in the Hauts-de-Seine department, at the south-western edge of the Paris agglomeration. The town is often described as a garden-town, and the label is well deserved: Chaville is framed by two great national forests, the Foret de Meudon to the south and the Foret de Fausses-Reposes to the north-west. Together, these two wooded areas cover several hundred hectares of oaks, hornbeams and beeches, offering residents immediate access to nature. Few communes in the inner Paris suburbs can boast such a natural environment.

The Etangs d'Ursine, on the edge of the Foret de Fausses-Reposes, are one of Chaville's natural jewels. This protected site, where ponds, reedbeds and damp woodlands intermingle, is a popular walking spot and a precious habitat for wildlife. The proximity of these natural spaces gives Chaville a distinctive atmosphere, halfway between town and countryside.

The town centre has undergone a renovation programme giving it a more modern and pleasant appearance. Local shops cluster around the station square. The Chaville market is a weekly fixture. The town also benefits from its proximity to Versailles and the Velizy 2 shopping centre. The urban landscape is dominated by detached houses and small residential buildings. Families are numerous, attracted by the quality of the environment and the tranquillity of the neighbourhoods.

Orthodontic treatments for patients from Chaville

Clear aligners are the most popular treatment among adult patients and appearance-conscious teenagers from Chaville. These transparent, custom-made trays correct dental alignment gradually without anyone noticing. For residents who work at La Defense or in Paris, this discretion is a decisive advantage. Aligners are removed for meals and toothbrushing. Check-up appointments are spaced eight to twelve weeks apart. A digital simulation allows the patient to visualise the expected result step by step.

Multi-bracket braces remain the reference technique for complex corrections. For teenagers from Chaville, multi-bracket braces are often the most appropriate choice when significant tooth movements are needed. Metal brackets offer precise movement control. Ceramic brackets provide an excellent aesthetic alternative.

Interceptive orthodontics is for children aged six to nine. Families in Chaville, where the proportion of young children is significant, are often attentive to early signs and consult promptly. Interceptive treatment typically lasts a few months and can prevent more extensive treatment during adolescence.

Adult orthodontics represents a growing proportion of her activity. Executives and professionals from Chaville, who commute daily, seek discreet and effective solutions. Modern techniques treat adults effectively while accounting for the particularities of their dentition.

Treatment process for a patient from Chaville

The first step is the assessment consultation at the Colombes practice. Dr Chardon carefully examines the patient's teeth, takes photographs, and discusses expectations, goals and any apprehensions. She explains the various treatment options. If treatment is indicated, she prescribes a panoramic radiograph, cephalometric radiograph and 3D digital impressions.

The treatment plan is presented at a second appointment, detailing the recommended technique, estimated duration, follow-up frequency, total cost and coverage options. For a patient from Chaville, she points out that the journey takes no more than thirty minutes by public transport or fifteen minutes by car.

During active treatment, each check-up allows her to verify progress, adjust the appliance and answer all questions. Her educational and transparent approach makes patients active participants in their treatment.

At the end of active treatment, the retention phase takes over. A bonded wire or night-time retainer maintains teeth in position. Regular retention check-ups verify long-term stability. Her commitment to patients from Chaville extends well beyond the removal of the appliance.
