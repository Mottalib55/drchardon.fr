Orthodontic treatments for patients from Asnieres-sur-Seine

Her practice in Colombes offers every major orthodontic treatment modality. Interceptive orthodontics is aimed at children between roughly six and eleven years old, addressing problems like crossbites, underbites, and severe crowding before they become entrenched. Early treatment can guide jaw growth, create space for erupting permanent teeth, and reduce the severity of any later treatment that may be needed.

Multi-bracket braces are the most versatile treatment option and are particularly well suited to the complex malocclusions that teenagers often present with. she uses self-ligating bracket systems that produce efficient tooth movement with reduced discomfort. Tooth-coloured ceramic brackets are available for patients who prefer a more discreet look. For adults from Asnieres-sur-Seine, clear aligners are frequently the preferred option. These transparent, removable trays straighten teeth progressively and are barely visible during wear. she plans each aligner treatment digitally from start to finish, which allows patients to see their projected results before treatment begins.

Regardless of age or technique, every treatment she delivers follows the same principles: a precise diagnosis, a carefully designed plan, and meticulous execution. Her aim is always a result that is stable, functional, and aesthetically pleasing.
