Orthodontics Nearby Western Paris

Dr Chardon's practice in Colombes is directly connected to the western arrondissements of Paris by the Transilien from the Gare Saint-Lazare.

An orthodontic practice on the doorstep of western Paris

Dr Sarah Chardon's orthodontic practice is located in Colombes (92700) and welcomes patients from the western arrondissements of Paris. The practice is near the Gare du Stade station (Transilien L), roughly 20 minutes by direct train from the Gare Saint-Lazare.

Residents of the 16th, 17th, 8th, 9th, 18th, 7th, 1st and 15th arrondissements can reach the practice by train from Saint-Lazare or by car via the Porte de Champerret, Porte Maillot or Porte de Clichy.

How to get here from Paris

By Transilien from the Gare Saint-Lazare

The Transilien L line runs directly from the Gare Saint-Lazare to the Gare du Stade in Colombes. The journey takes approximately 15 minutes. Trains run frequently during peak hours and throughout the day. The Gare Saint-Lazare is itself served by the Metro (lines 3, 9, 12, 13, 14) and numérous bus routes, making it accèssible from all western arrondissements.

From the 17th arrondissement

The 17th is the closest arrondissement to Colombes. By car, take the Porte de Champerret or the Porte de Clichy to reach the practice in 15 to 20 minutes depending on traffic. By Metro and Transilien, take Line 13 to the Gare Saint-Lazare, then the Transilien L.

From the 16th and 8th arrondissements

From the 16th, the Porte Maillot and the boulevard peripherique provide accèss to Colombes in about 20 minutes. From the 8th, the Gare Saint-Lazare is just a few minutes away by Metro or on foot, making the total journey to Colombes very quick.

From the 9th and 18th arrondissements

The 9th arrondissement is close to Saint-Lazare and offers direct accèss to the Transilien. From the 18th, reach Saint-Lazare via Metro Line 12 (Jules Joffrin to Saint-Lazare in 15 minutes) or take the Transilien J directly from the Gare du Nord.

From the 7th, 1st and 15th arrondissements

These arrondissements are a little further away but remain well served. Metro Line 12 (Assemblee Nationale to Saint-Lazare) or Line 14 (Pyramides to Saint-Lazare) provide quick connections to the Transilien. By car, allow 25 to 30 minutes depending on traffic conditions.
