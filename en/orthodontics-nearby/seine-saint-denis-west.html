Orthodontics Nearby Western Seine-Saint-Denis

Dr Chardon's practice in Colombes sits right at the border with Seine-Saint-Denis. Quick accèss from Saint-Ouen, Saint-Denis, Epinay and neighbouring cities.

An orthodontic practice close to western Seine-Saint-Denis

Dr Sarah Chardon's practice is located in Colombes (92700), at the boundary between the Hauts-de-Seine and Seine-Saint-Denis departments. The practice welcomes residents from the western part of the Seine-Saint-Denis (department 93).

The cities of Saint-Ouen-sur-Seine, Saint-Denis, L'Ile-Saint-Denis, Epinay-sur-Seine, La Courneuve, Stains, Pierrefitte-sur-Seine and Villetaneuse are all within 10 to 20 minutes by car from the practice.

Getting here from western Seine-Saint-Denis

From L'Ile-Saint-Denis and Gennevilliers

L'Ile-Saint-Denis, a small island community on the Seine, is the closest city to the practice on the Seine-Saint-Denis side. The drive takes only about 8 minutes via the Pont de l'Ile-Saint-Denis and Gennevilliers. The practice is open to all residents of this area.

From Saint-Ouen-sur-Seine

Saint-Ouen, now officially Saint-Ouen-sur-Seine, is reachable in around 12 minutes by car via the D911 and the Pont de Gennevilliers. By public transport, residents can take Metro Line 13 or Line 14 to Saint-Lazare, then the Transilien L to Colombes. The total journey takes about 30 minutes.

From Saint-Denis and Epinay-sur-Seine

Saint-Denis, the departmental capital, is roughly 15 minutes away by car via the A86 or the D311 through Gennevilliers. Epinay-sur-Seine, located north of Saint-Denis along the Seine, is a similar distance. The Transilien H from Epinay can be combined with the Transilien L to reach the practice.

From La Courneuve, Stains and Pierrefitte

These cities further east in the Seine-Saint-Denis remain within a manâgeable distance. La Courneuve is approximately 18 minutes away via the A86. Stains and Pierrefitte-sur-Seine can be reached in about 20 minutes by car via the D30 and then the A86. The T5 tramway serves Pierrefitte and allows connections to the Colombes transport network.

From Villetaneuse

Villetaneuse, known for its university campus, is about 20 minutes from the practice. Students and residents can reach Colombes by the Transilien H with a connection, or directly by car via Saint-Denis and the A86.
